The short version

Zonette collects nothing, sends nothing and has no network access.

What is stored, and where

Inside the app's own sandbox container on your Mac: your cities, the names you give them, and your settings. Deleting the app removes them.

Your calendar

Only if you switch on Show my next events, Zonette asks macOS for access to your calendars. It then reads the title, time and colour of your next events, on your Mac, to show them with their time in your cities. It never changes an event, never stores them, and never sends them anywhere.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Zonette has no network entitlement, which means macOS itself will not let it open a network connection. The list of cities and the map are part of the app. There is no server, no analytics and no account.
