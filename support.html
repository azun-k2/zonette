Email khangazun@gmail.com. The link opens a message with a short template in it. The Email the developer button in Settings ▸ About fills in your macOS version and your cities' time zones for you.

Common questions

I can't find my city

Zonette knows every city of 15,000 people or more. Try another spelling, or the country's name, which lists its biggest cities first. For a smaller place, add the nearest city in the same time zone and rename it.

How does the meeting planner decide who is at work?

By the working hours in Settings ▸ Cities, 9:00 to 18:00 unless you change them, the same in every city, on weekdays. Drag across the strips or click one to choose a time; Copy puts that time in every city on the clipboard.

My events do not show

Switch on Show my next events in Settings ▸ Calendar and allow calendar access when macOS asks. If you declined, allow Zonette in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Calendars. All-day events are left out, since they have no time to show in another city.

The times disappeared from the menu bar

When the menu bar is full, macOS moves some items behind its overflow arrow. Shorter city names help: give a city a nickname in Settings ▸ Cities, or switch off Show the city names in Settings ▸ Menu Bar.

Does it handle daylight saving time?

Yes. Every time comes from macOS's own time zone rules, including the dates each country changes its clocks.
